Out on mission

Where we have been, and where we are going next

This is the honest record of what we actually do: the streets we knocked on, the tables we set up in a park, the evenings we spent in someone's living room. If something here is near you, come. You do not need an invitation and you do not need to be an Adventist.

Is there a need on your street?

A neighbour nobody checks on. A family that lost everything in the last storm. A block where nobody has ever been offered a Bible study. Tell us and we will come — you do not have to organise anything, and you do not have to be there when we do.
